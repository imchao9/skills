# Commentary Style

## Structure

Use this structure for most Xiaoqiumi post-game reviews:

1. Title with winner, score, and core story.
2. Opening paragraph with match context and overall rhythm.
3. Winner section covering why the winning team controlled the game.
4. Losing team section covering leaders, resistance, and what still deserves respect.
5. Key data section covering rebounds, assists, shooting, turnovers, and rotation depth.
6. Closing复盘 with next-step outlook.

## Tone

Write like a local basketball recap, not a pro-league wire report.
The article should be readable in a WeChat group and should make players feel accurately seen.

Use exact player names, jersey numbers, and stats.
Mention role players when the data shows real contribution.
Do not force dramatic language if the numbers suggest a steady win.

## Data-To-Narrative Mapping

Use scoring leaders to identify main offensive points.
Use rebound margin to explain second-chance chances and possession pressure.
Use assists to judge whether the team relied on individual play or shared the ball.
Use turnovers to explain missed chances, but avoid blaming one player unless the data and user context make it clear.
Use three-point and free-throw lines to explain efficiency swings.

## Avoid

Do not invent injuries.
Do not invent coach decisions.
Do not invent conflicts.
Do not claim a comeback or collapse unless the period scores support it.
Do not say “数据无法体现” too often.
Use it only when describing effort, defense, or rotation pressure that was visible from user-provided context.
