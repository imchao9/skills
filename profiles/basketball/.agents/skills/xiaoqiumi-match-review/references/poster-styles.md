# Poster Styles

## cinematic-dark

Best for dramatic game recap posters.
Use a court photo as a full-bleed background, dark overlay, oversized score, and short article excerpt.

## scoreboard-card

Best when the scoreboard photo is important.
Put the final score and scoreboard image in the first visual hierarchy, then place leaders and team stats in compact cards.

## newspaper

Best for text-heavy reviews.
Use a white editorial layout, strong headline, byline, article excerpt, and data sidebar.

## neon-energy

Best for a promotional social poster.
Use dark background, bright cyan/red/yellow accents, diagonal blocks, and short punchy labels.

## minimal-clean

Best for formal summaries.
Use a light background, strict grid, compact statistics, and limited decoration.

## Text Accuracy Rule

For Chinese text, deterministic HTML is usually safer than direct image generation.
Use gpt-image for atmospheric background or illustration only when exact names and scores can remain in HTML overlay.
